What Is PP Corrugated Plastic Sheet?

PP corrugated plastic sheet — also called twin-wall corrugated PP sheet, coroplast sheet, or flute board — is a flat-panel material manufactured from polypropylene using a continuous extrusion process. The result is a rigid, lightweight sheet with a twin-wall cross-section: two flat face skins connected by regularly spaced internal ribs running parallel to one another through the panel. This structure gives PP corrugated sheet exceptional stiffness per unit weight, and the enclosed air channels provide a measure of thermal insulation and cushioning.

Unlike paper-based corrugated or fiberboard panels, PP corrugated sheet does not absorb water, does not soften when wet, and does not degrade under repeated handling. A single PP corrugated sheet used as a pallet layer divider can replace 50, 100, or more single-use cardboard layer pads over its service life. For high-throughput distribution operations that consume thousands of cardboard layer pads per week, switching to reusable PP corrugated sheet represents a significant and recurring cost reduction.

Corrugated plastic sheet is available in a broad range of gauges — from 2mm thin-wall for lightweight applications like signage and box liners to 10mm heavy-wall for structural pallet layer pads, dividers in metal fabrication, and industrial surface protection. Sheet sizes range from standard 48×96 in (4×8 ft) panels, which are compatible with standard pallet footprints and fabrication workflows, to custom-cut sheet in any rectangular dimension.

"A 4mm PP corrugated sheet used as a pallet layer pad weighs roughly 60 to 70 percent less than a standard cardboard layer pad of the same surface area, and it handles 50 or more reuse cycles — delivering the lowest per-use cost of any layer-pad material on the market."

Signage & Point-of-Sale

Custom Cut Sizes and Fabrication

Standard 48×96 in sheets can be cut to any rectangular dimension using standard woodworking or sheet metal tooling. PP corrugated sheet scores and creases cleanly for fold-up box construction, tray fabrication, and tabbed inserts. Die-cut parts in custom shapes are available for high-volume applications where precision repeatability is required.

Common custom cut sizes for pallet layer pad applications include 40×48 in (GMA pallet standard), 42×42 in, 48×40 in, and 36×36 in. Custom sizes are cut from 48×96 in parent sheets at minimal or no setup cost for rectangular cuts.
