ESD and Anti-Static Corrugate Sheets: A Buying Guide for Electronics Handling

Corrugatesheet Editorial • November 2026

Why Static Protection Matters in Corrugate Selection

For buyers packaging electronic assemblies, printed circuit boards, or sensitive components, standard polypropylene corrugate can create a hidden liability. PP is naturally insulative and can generate and hold a static charge during handling, which risks electrostatic discharge damage to the very products the packaging is meant to protect. Specifying the correct static-control grade is therefore a core part of the buying decision, not an afterthought. This guide outlines what procurement teams need to request and verify.

The static-control family breaks into three functional grades, each defined by surface resistance measured in ohms per square. Understanding the distinctions prevents both under-protection and overspending on a grade the application does not require.

Understanding the Three Grades

  • Anti-static (dissipative at the high end): surface resistance typically in the 10^9 to 10^11 ohm range. Prevents charge generation from handling and rubbing. Suitable for general component packaging where the main risk is triboelectric buildup.
  • Static dissipative: resistance in the 10^6 to 10^9 ohm range. Allows controlled, slow bleed of charge to ground. The common choice for most electronics work-in-process packaging.
  • Conductive: resistance below 10^6 ohms. Provides a fast path to ground and Faraday-cage shielding when constructed as a tote or box. Used for the most sensitive devices.

Match the grade to the device sensitivity classification and the handling environment. Over-specifying conductive material for a low-risk part inflates cost; under-specifying leaves sensitive devices exposed.

Permanent vs Topical Treatments

Anti-static performance is delivered either through a topical surface treatment or through additives compounded into the resin. Topical treatments are lower cost but can wear off with washing, abrasion, and time, and their effectiveness varies with ambient humidity. Compounded or permanent static-control grades hold their properties through wash-down cycles and across humidity ranges, which matters for reusable, returnable packaging programs. For closed-loop electronics logistics, the permanent grade almost always wins on cost-per-trip despite the higher unit price.

When evaluating suppliers, request documented surface-resistance test data and ask whether the rating is topical or inherent. A specification without test documentation is a specification you cannot defend during an audit.

Construction and Fabrication Options

Static-control corrugate is available as flat sheet stock, die-cut inserts, dividers, and formed totes. Buyers frequently combine a tote built from PP corrugated boxes with internal dividers to isolate individual components within a Faraday-protected outer shell.
